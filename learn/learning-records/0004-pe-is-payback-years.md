# P/E is the number of years of today's profit that equals the price; it describes the price and doesn't measure growth

The owner understood P/E as payback years and then worked out, before being told, that P/E is not an indicator of price (it's calculated from the price) and not an indicator of growth (growth shows in profit over the years).

**Evidence:** "Shop B is growing and people are paying more for Shop A" (correct on both).
