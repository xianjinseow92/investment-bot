# Owning pays through dividends, buybacks, a takeover, or selling, and no dividend doesn't mean no value

The owner can list the four routes by which a company's profit reaches its owners and sees retained profit as "dividends delayed, not cancelled". Misconception corrected: a buyback doesn't pay you unless you sell into it; if you keep your shares, your slice gets bigger. The owner's earlier worry ("if it never pays, isn't it just people betting on people?") was correct in principle and is now resolved.

**Evidence:** the owner's own summary of the four routes, unprompted.
