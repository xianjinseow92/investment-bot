# The price only changes when a trade happens, at wherever eager buyers and sellers meet

The owner stated this themselves before it was taught, and rejected an explanation that blurred profit and price ("worth more"). Now understands the two queues: the next trade happens when someone accepts the other side's best price, so a rush of buyers pushes trades up and a rush of sellers pushes them down. Future lessons must never imply a price "updates" by itself.

**Evidence:** "Eve pays $2,600"; "price falls because people sell cheaper to get rid of their stock".
