# A share is an ownership claim, and trades between investors don't involve the company

The owner understands that a share is a recorded piece of ownership (like a title deed), that only the first sale sends money to the company, and that later trades are between investors. Corrected along the way: owning a share is not "a piece at that point in time" but a claim on every future year for as long as it's held, which is the foundation for why future profits matter.

**Evidence:** answered "who pays you when you sell?" correctly ("the person who buys the stock from me"); asked the right follow-up ("does the money actually go to her?").
