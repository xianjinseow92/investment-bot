# Investing basics Glossary

Terms the owner has shown they understand, in the words we use for them. Running example: Mei's bubble tea shop.

## Owning a company

**Share**:
One piece of ownership of a company, held as a record (not a physical thing); a claim on a slice of everything the company owns and earns.
_Avoid_: Stock (for a single piece), equity, piece (fine in examples)

**Stock market**:
The marketplace where existing shares are bought and sold between people; the company is not part of these trades.
_Avoid_: The exchange (for the whole market)

**Dividend**:
Cash a company pays to its owners out of profit, split equally per share.

**Buyback**:
A company buying its own shares and cancelling them, so each remaining share is a bigger slice of the company.

**Takeover**:
Another company buying every share of a company, paying each owner the agreed price per share.
_Avoid_: Buyout, acquisition (same thing; use one word)

## Prices

**Price**:
The price of the most recent trade; it changes only when a new trade happens at a different price.
_Avoid_: Value, worth (a price is not what something is "worth")

**Bid**:
The highest price someone in the buying queue is currently offering.

**Ask**:
The lowest price someone in the selling queue is currently willing to accept.

**Order**:
An instruction to buy or sell a number of shares, at a price or at whatever the queue offers.

**Fill**:
An order, or part of one, that actually happened, at a known price.
_Avoid_: Trade (for an order that hasn't happened)

## Valuation

**Profit per share**:
A company's yearly profit divided by its number of shares.
_Avoid_: EPS (fine as an abbreviation once used), earnings (when the per-share figure is meant)

**P/E ratio**:
Price ÷ profit per share: how many years of today's profit it takes to equal the price. Describes the price; does not measure growth.
