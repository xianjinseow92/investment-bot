# Averaging down doesn't reduce a loss; it puts more money on the price recovering

Came up live when GOOGL fell ~9% after hours on Alphabet's Q3 2026 results and the owner (holding 12 shares at $308.80) wanted to "buy the discount" because it was below their average price. Worked through a table: buying 12 more at $290 and selling later changes nothing if the price is flat, helps only if it rises, and doubles the damage if it falls. The owner concluded "you're actually spending more", i.e. a bigger bet. Also covered: the price doesn't know your average (anchoring), and "it fell, so it's a discount" assumes the old price was right. The real question is whether you'd have bought at the old price.

**Evidence:** the owner's own summary after the table. Not yet shown: the follow-up check (why you lose more at $280).
