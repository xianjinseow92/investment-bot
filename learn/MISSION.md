# Mission: Investing basics

**Status: draft, written by Claude from the conversation on 28 Oct 2026; owner to confirm or change.**

## Why
Build and supervise a personal trading bot without being at its mercy: know what it is doing with money, tell whether its results are skill or luck, and decide with confidence when (or whether) real money goes in. Underneath that: stop feeling that the financial system is something that can't be grasped.

## Success looks like
- Explain in your own words what a share is, how its price moves, and how owning one pays you.
- Read the bot's daily message and explain every order and why its rule fired.
- Compare a strategy with the benchmark: return after costs, drawdown, and whether the gap could be luck.
- Spot at least three ways a backtest can mislead.
- Make the real-money decision from the written criteria, not from a good month.

## Constraints
- One idea at a time, with a running example (Mei's bubble tea shop). Mechanisms, not just definitions.
- Short lessons; answer the check questions before opening new ones.
- Learning happens alongside building the bot; it does not block it.

## Out of scope (for now)
- Margin and options (excluded from the bot's version 1; come back later as their own topic).
- Picking individual companies by analysing them; day trading; crypto.
