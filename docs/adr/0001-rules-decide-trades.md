# Fixed rules decide every trade; AI never does

Every order comes from a strategy written as fixed, tested code. An AI model may explain, summarise or flag things, but never decides an order. We chose this over letting an AI model read news and prices and decide, because a strategy has to be tested on past prices before it is trusted, and an AI model already knows what happened in the past, so a test of its decisions on past prices would flatter it. Fixed rules can also be repeated exactly and explained trade by trade, which helps the owner learn. (Agreed 28 Oct 2026.)
